# PupilCard

The big, colourful navigation tile of the Pupil Zone (children and young people). One large icon, a short title and one sentence; the whole tile is the link.

## Props
`href`, `title`, `text`, `icon`, `tone` (`'teal' | 'yellow' | 'coral' | 'purple'`).

## Pupil Zone
Wrap the young people's section in `data-zone="pupil"` (the prototype's `PupilLayout`): body copy steps up to 21/30, headings to 30/36, cards and panels take `radius-lg`, and the young-people accent applies throughout. Write to "you", one idea per sentence, and define every acronym with `Term`.
