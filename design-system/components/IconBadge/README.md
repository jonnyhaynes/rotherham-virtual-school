# IconBadge

An icon on a solid brand-colour disc or rounded square — the audience markers and card icons.

## Props
`icon`, `tone` (`'teal' | 'coral' | 'yellow' | 'purple' | 'navy' | 'pale' | 'error' | 'success'`), `size` (`'sm' 32 | 'md' 48 | 'lg' 64`), `shape` (`'circle' | 'square'`), `label` (makes it meaningful; otherwise decorative).

## Usage
Glyphs are `navy` on teal (4.7:1), yellow (9.2:1), coral (4.7:1) and purple (4.9:1); white on navy. Use the audience tone and icon for audience content: teal/user for children and young people, coral/users for parents and carers, yellow/school for schools, purple/clipboard for social workers, navy/briefcase for professionals.
