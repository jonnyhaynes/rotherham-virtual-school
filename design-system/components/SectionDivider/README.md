# SectionDivider

Branded breaks between page sections: `hills` (the rolling landscape from the logo and illustrations), `pattern` (a single row of the brand pattern) and `rule` (a short teal bar under a heading).

## Props
`variant` (`'hills' | 'pattern' | 'rule'`), `height` (hills, 64), `flip` (hills upside down, to open a section), `seed` (pattern).

## Usage
Hills sit between a white section and a pale-blue one, or at the foot of a home hero. Use at most one hills divider per page. The rule sits under a statement heading, as on the brand board.
